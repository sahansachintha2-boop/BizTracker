Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

BizTrack is a personal and small-business money management app developed by Sahan Gunawardana (“we”). This policy explains what information BizTrack handles and how you stay in control of it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details – your name, email address and profile photo. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive only your name, email address and profile photo from that service. We never receive your Google or Facebook password.
  • Financial records you enter – income, expenses, accounts, customers, suppliers, loans, budgets, savings goals, and any notes.
  • Receipts you attach – photos or PDF files you choose to upload.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and BizTrack contains no advertising or tracking.

2. How we use it

Only to provide the app to you: to sign you in, save your records, and keep them in sync across your devices. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone for marketing.

3. Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • Your data is stored on your device and in a secure cloud database operated by Supabase (Singapore region).
  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Every record is tied to your account, and the database only allows the signed-in owner to read or change it. Other users cannot see your data.
  • When you sign out, the copy of your data on that device is removed.

4. Service providers

Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Google and Facebook (only if you choose them to sign in), and GitHub Pages (hosting the app). These providers process data only to deliver their services.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all its data at any time — see Delete my data below.

6. Children

BizTrack is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Delete my data

Option 1 – in the app (instant):

  1. Open BizTrack and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → My account.
  3. Tap Delete account and all data, then type DELETE to confirm.

Your account, all financial records and all uploaded receipts are permanently deleted from our servers immediately.

Option 2 – by email: send a request from your account email address to Sahansachintha2@gmail.com with the subject “BizTrack data deletion request”. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove BizTrack from Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
